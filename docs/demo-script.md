# Selkie demo video script

A tight, confident walkthrough that makes a judge believe this is real and ready. Target length
**2 minutes 30 seconds** (a 60 second teaser cut is at the bottom). The spine of the whole video is
one promise, shown in the first ten seconds and never dropped:

> **Send real money to a name, in seconds, and keep it private. On Canton.**

## Before you hit record

- Use **two handles you control** (yours plus a spare on X, and the same on Telegram) so you can show
  both sides of a payment. The receiver in the script is `@ada`. Swap in your real spare handle.
- Fund your wallet first so balances are not empty on camera. Open the dashboard, check CC, USDCx,
  cBTC and cETH show real numbers.
- Do a full dry run once. The X reply takes about ten seconds to land, so know that beat is coming
  and do not talk over the dead air. Cut it tighter in the edit.
- Everything must be **real**. No mocks, no fake balances. That is the entire point, and judges can
  tell. If a live send is risky on the day, pre-record the real send and keep the clip.
- Keep the cursor calm. Slow, deliberate moves read as confident. Fast jittery moves read as nervous.

## Delivery notes

- Read the voice track a little slower than feels natural. Let the product moments breathe.
- The two lines that must land clean are the first one and the last one. Rehearse those most.
- If you only get one thing perfect, get the cold open perfect. A real payment to a walletless handle
  in the first ten seconds is the whole pitch.
